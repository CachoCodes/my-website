Tume-Arandu
Kotlin · Android · Education

An offline-first Android learning app for introductory trigonometry in Guaraní, with an optional Spanish translation. Interactive exercises and progress are stored on the device.

The Guaraní course text is an early draft and should be reviewed by a Guaraní-speaking math teacher.
